# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

This project explicitly invites disagreement about the technical content — see `CONTRIBUTING.md`. Disagreement about *ideas* is welcome and encouraged. Disagreement conducted with contempt for the *person* is not, and will be moderated regardless of how correct the underlying technical point turns out to be.

Examples of behavior that helps this project:
- Citing the specific sub-model, section, or schema you're referring to.
- Proposing a concrete counter-example over an abstract objection.
- Disagreeing plainly, without needing to soften a correct technical point.
- Accepting correction gracefully when a counter-example holds up.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:
- Personal attacks, insults, or derogatory comments, in this repository or elsewhere in connection with it.
- Harassment of any kind, public or private.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.

## Scope

This applies within all project spaces — GitHub Issues, Discussions, Pull Requests — and in public spaces when an individual is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported privately by emailing **conduct@lunex.cloud**. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated, and will result in a response deemed necessary and appropriate to the circumstances. Maintainers are obligated to maintain confidentiality with regard to the reporter of an incident.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this Code of Conduct in good faith may face temporary or permanent repercussions, as determined by other members of the project's leadership.
